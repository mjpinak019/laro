# Bahay-bahayan — advice only, not implemented

This is feasible, and the existing coins, levels, quests and save backups
provide a good foundation. It is a substantial feature: building needs an
inventory, placement rules, touch controls, collision handling, upgrades and
save migration in addition to artwork. Keep its first version small.

Start with one marked community play lot. Use preset building slots rather
than free placement across the barangay, keeping doors, roads, game courts
and quest routes clear. Offer a rotate/preview/confirm interaction, with
large phone controls. A preview must never spend materials.

Suggested materials are clean cardboard, washed plastic bottles, paper
tubes, fabric offcuts and reusable bamboo pieces. Materials can come from
completed cleanup quests, capped random discoveries, or a coin shop. Reward
returning lost items separately; do not encourage taking other people's
property for crafting. Use clean, safe materials suited to pretend play.

| Suggested level | Preset build | Added detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Cardboard playhouse | Walls, door and cloth roof |
| 10 | Bottle planters and fence | Colored plants and woven texture |
| 15 | Decorated bahay-kubo-inspired playhouse | Raised pretend floor, bamboo trim |
| 20 | Community reading nook | Reused crates and book shelves |
| 25–30 | Fiesta makeover | Parol decorations and a shared play table |

Track materials and placed parts in the same player save, with stable item
IDs and maximum inventory counts. Refund materials when a player removes
a build; charge only for an explicit new craft or upgrade. Preserve old
saves before migration. Offline or local play can use the same save model;
shared multiplayer lots would require server ownership, permissions and
authoritative spending checks.

Useful future quests could ask the player to return a borrowed tool, sort
three material types, water a planter, lend a seat at the reading nook, or
help decorate a community event. Let players choose how to help. Avoid
requiring chores to enter their already-unlocked games.

The recommended next step is a small prototype with one lot, five materials
and three builds. No building, crafting inventory, material store or lot
placement has been added in alpha 0.14.
