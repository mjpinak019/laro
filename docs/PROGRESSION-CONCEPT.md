# Optional neighborhood progression — proposal only

No food-powered energy requirement, leveling system, elder-crossing event,
trash activity, or level-locked content is implemented in this release.
The existing sprint meter remains a short-term movement mechanic. Buying
snacks is an optional coin interaction and never grants permission to play.

## Recommendation

Keep core games available without an energy cost. Requiring food to start
another round could interrupt the collection's main attraction: one more
game with friends. It could also make a low-coin player feel forced to do
chores. Use food, errands, and exploration as optional reasons to visit the
map instead of requirements for playing.

A useful alternative is a neighborhood kindness/reputation track. Finish
games, discover places, help neighbors, or dispose of litter to earn modest
coins or experience. Offer several routes to the same rewards so a player
can keep doing what they enjoy. Food could later grant a small optional
temporary bonus or a scrapbook collectible, without reducing the baseline
experience for players who do not buy it.

An elder-crossing opportunity about every ten minutes can be a charming
occasional event, provided it is optional. Mark it clearly when available,
let the elder cross safely without the player, and keep other activities
available. Avoid forcing players to wait for a timer to progress. Trash
disposal can offer another small activity, with limits on repeated rewards.

## Levels and unlocks

Levels could make the barangay feel like a place the player belongs to.
Award experience for playing, learning, exploring, and helping. Good unlocks
include clothing, cosmetic variants, decorations, side stories, and hidden
areas with extra interactions. Show the next reward and a clear progress bar.

Keep the main games open. Optional challenge variants and bonus courts can
unlock later. Prefer alternate paths to cosmetics (a level milestone OR
coins), rather than making players satisfy both a long grind and a purchase.
Difficulty and skill rewards should never depend on buying food or clothes.

Start small if this proposal is approved: a few reputation milestones,
one helpful-neighbor activity, and one discoverable area. Playtest that loop
before adding food bonuses, more currencies, or a large level tree.
