# Merienda artwork references

The menu SVGs are original simplified drawings, retaining LARO's muted palette
and dark outlines. Reference photographs are not bundled, traced as exact
compositions, or hotlinked by the game. All artwork works offline.

| Menu item | Visual correction | Photo/product reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fishball | Pale, slightly flattened fried fish cakes on a bamboo skewer, with a separate sauce cup | [Philippine Department of Tourism street-food photo gallery](https://philippinetourism.co.kr/phil-intro/street-foods), Fish Balls photo |
| Kwek-kwek | Orange battered eggs; a cut piece shows the white and yolk | [DOT gallery](https://philippinetourism.co.kr/phil-intro/street-foods), Kwek-kwek; [Kawaling Pinoy](https://www.kawalingpinoy.com/kwek-kwek/) |
| Kikiam | Brown elongated fried pieces, not round balls | [DOT gallery](https://philippinetourism.co.kr/phil-intro/street-foods), Kikiam photo |
| Taho | A cup of pale silken tofu, brown arnibal syrup, and sago pearls | [DOT gallery](https://philippinetourism.co.kr/phil-intro/street-foods), Taho; [Kawaling Pinoy](https://www.kawalingpinoy.com/taho/) |
| Biscuits | Square crackers with toasted edges and puncture holes, beside an unbranded snack pack | [Rebisco plain crackers product page](https://www.rebisco.com.ph/biscuits-and-crackers/15-rebisco-crackers.html) |
| Juice | A sealed drink pouch with a straw and mango illustration, rather than a skewer | [Zest-O mango product photo](https://zbga.shopsuki.ph/products/zest-o-juice-drink-mango-200ml) |

The final SVG geometry is in `client/src/food-art.ts`. Packaging is unbranded.
